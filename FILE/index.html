Barbell Back Squat

Target: Quadriceps, Glutes, Hamstrings, Core

  1. Set the barbell on your upper traps (not your neck) and grip just outside shoulder width.
  2. Stand with feet shoulder-width apart, toes pointed slightly outward.
  3. Brace your core, push your hips back, and bend your knees until thighs are parallel to the floor.
  4. Drive through your mid-foot to stand back up explosively.
